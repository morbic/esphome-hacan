= Hardware commissioning procedure
:toc: left
:sectnums:

This procedure validates the initial ESPHome HACAN component on two physical
ESP32 nodes. It validates commissioning and the volatile entity directory; it
does not yet validate an ESPHome endpoint platform.

== Prerequisites

* Two ESP32 development boards with CAN transceivers and the physical bus
  wiring already prepared at 125 kbit/s.
* A serial connection to each board for ESPHome logs.
* Optionally, a passive CAN monitor such as a USB-CAN adapter for frame capture.
* ESPHome installed in the local shell.

The included examples use the ESP32 built-in CAN controller on `GPIO5` (TX)
and `GPIO4` (RX). Change these pins, the board, or the framework in both YAML
files when the physical hardware differs.

== Prepare the firmware

The example files load this repository's component through the standard
ESPHome local external-component mechanism:

[source,yaml]
----
external_components:
  - source:
      type: local
      path: ../components
----

The path is relative to the YAML file. Keep the YAML files in the repository's
`examples` directory, or adjust the path when copying them elsewhere.

Flash the commissioning manager first:

[source,shell]
----
esphome run examples/commissioning-primary.yaml --device /dev/ttyUSB0
----

Then flash the peer:

[source,shell]
----
esphome run examples/commissioning-peer.yaml --device /dev/ttyUSB1
----

Replace device paths with the serial ports assigned by the host. The primary
observes EntityId `0x01010001`; the peer owns the same EntityId at endpoint
`0x01`.

== Scenario 1: Cold start and address assignment

. Erase persistent storage on both boards. For a standard ESP32, use
  `esptool.py --chip esp32 --port /dev/ttyUSB0 erase_flash`, then repeat with
  the peer's serial port. On macOS, the port normally has the form
  `/dev/cu.usbserial-*` or `/dev/cu.usbmodem*`.
. Flash and power the primary alone. Its log must contain `Stored commissioned
  address: 0x001` and the HACAN configuration dump must show `0x001`.
. Power the peer. It first emits `DISCOVERY_REQUEST` and a delayed source-zero
  `DISCOVERY_RESPONSE`; the primary assigns the next free address.
. Confirm that the peer log contains `Stored commissioned address: 0x002`.
. Capture the two `ADDRESS_CLAIM` frames from each node. The primary claims
  `0x001`; the peer claims `0x002`.

Expected result: both addresses are persisted and no address-conflict log is
present.

== Scenario 2: Entity directory recovery

. Keep both nodes powered after Scenario 1.
. Confirm that the peer sends `ENTITY_CLAIM` for EntityId `0x01010001` and
  endpoint `0x01` after completing its address claims.
. The primary sends `ENTITY_RESOLVE` for that EntityId.
. Confirm the primary log reports `Entity 0x01010001 is available at 0x002
  endpoint 0x01`.

Expected result: the primary stores the peer's volatile entity mapping. The
same claim may be observed more than once; repeated claims are idempotent.

== Scenario 3: Restart and persisted address

. Power-cycle only the peer without erasing its flash.
. Confirm it reuses `0x002`, sends two `ADDRESS_CLAIM` frames, and does not wait
  for a new assignment.
. Confirm that entity availability returns after its fresh `ENTITY_CLAIM`.

Expected result: a normal restart does not alter either node's address.

== Scenario 4: Offline detection and replacement

. Disconnect or power off the peer.
. Wait at least 105 seconds after its last valid frame.
. Confirm that the primary log reports `Entity 0x01010001 is unavailable`.
. Flash a replacement peer configuration that owns `0x01010001`, connect it,
  and allow it to complete commissioning.
. Confirm that the primary logs a new available mapping for the replacement
  address.

Expected result: entity mappings are RAM-only, expire after the offline timeout,
and are rebuilt from a fresh claim.

== Scenario 5: Address conflict

. Keep the primary running at `0x001`.
. Program a second primary only for this isolated test, then connect it to the
  same bus.
. Observe an `ADDRESS_CONFLICT` and confirm that the node with the numerically
  higher NodeUID withdraws and stops normal traffic.

Do not operate two primary nodes outside this controlled conflict test.
